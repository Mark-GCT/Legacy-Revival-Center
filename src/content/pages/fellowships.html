Global Fellowships

The Great Commission isn't a suggestion. We are actively planting and partnering with revival hubs across the globe.

Uganda

Legacy Revival Fellowship of Uganda

Lead by Apostle Edward & Jackie, Legacy Revival Fellowship of Uganda seek to dig deep wells of revival, support orphanages & surrounding communities, and plant vibrant Spirit-filled churches and spread the Gospel of Jesus Christ to all of Uganda.

Argentina

Legacy Revival Fellowship of Argentina

Fanning the flames of the Argentine revival heritage, Pastors Jose & Florencia are feeding the poor, reaching the unreached, and planting revival churches all throughout Argentina.

Nepal

Legacy Revival Fellowship of Nepal

Reaching the unreached in the Himalayas. Lead by Pastor Salomon Lama, Legacy Revival Fellowship of Nepal is planting churches and spreading the Gospel of Jesus to every remote corner of Nepal.
