Our Leadership

Our team is dedicated to serving the congregation and facilitating an atmosphere where God can move freely.

Pastors Dan & Cindy Duff

Lead Pastors

Pastors Dan & Cindy have pastored Legacy Revival Center since 2005. We are a church not satisfied with status quo Christianity but are pursuing and expecting a mighty outpouring of God's Spirit upon the face of the earth. Our desire is for a revival in our world; knowing that only a revival will change the moral compass of nations. Our heart's desire is to see the Bride of Christ in all her glory! We have a mandate upon us to raise up and invest in the next generation to carry the fire of the Holy Spirit to the nations. We owe the world a REVIVAL!

Mike & Melissa Williams

Associate Pastors

Michael and Melissa Williams have been apart of Legacy Revival Center for 23 years. Their heart is full of compassion and love for people. Being like Jesus is their goal. Michael and Melissa serve in many areas of the church. Outside the church Mike enjoys working out and spending time with his family. Melissa loves history, the outdoors and spending time with family.

Nick & Hailey Kaiser

Youth Pastors

Our heart is simple: we want to see young people genuinely know and love Jesus. We believe the teenage years are some of the most important years in a person's life, and it's a privilege to walk alongside students as they grow in their faith and discover the purpose God has for them.

More than anything, we want to create a place where students can encounter the presence of God through authentic worship, biblical teaching, and a community that encourages them to pursue Jesus wholeheartedly. Whether they've been in church their whole life or they're walking through the doors for the very first time, our prayer is that every student leaves knowing Jesus more deeply.
